How a call is made
  • Line: consensus from scoresandodds.com (midpoint of the best over and under).
  • Form %: 60% season hit rate + 40% last-5 hit rate over the line.
  • Projected %: 75% Form % + 25% the Vegas % of the over. ▲ OVER (green) 58%+, ◆ LEAN (yellow) 46–57%, ▼ UNDER (red) 45% or less. STRONG = 70%+ over or 30% or less under.
  • Line moves: ▲/▼ under the line shows how far it moved since the board first saw it today. Rising usually means money on the over. The board refreshes hourly 10 AM–8 PM ET.
  • Game lines: each game header shows a call on the total (how often each team's games went over that number, 60% season + 40% last 5, plus 25% Vegas) and on the spread (how often the away team would have covered and the home team failed to). Same colors; the spread names the likelier side.
  • Daily Sheet tab: a one-page sheet of today's game-line calls, green overs and strong unders, printable and emailed daily.
  • Anytime TD: priced from Vegas alone and marked low confidence. 0.5 steals and blocks lines are left off.
  • NFL matchup: what today's defense allows this season to the player's position for that stat, ranked 1 (toughest) to 32. It nudges Form % up to 8 points either way.
  • Vegas %: the chance the over price implies. It makes up a quarter of Projected %.
  • Tap a name: career games vs today's opponent. 🔥 = over in 60%+ of 3+ games. Reference only.
  • Injuries: ⚠️ questionable or day-to-day. OUT, doubtful or injured reserve players are greyed out with no call.
  • Penny Blocks tab: five parlays per sport from the highest projected overs, with links to build them on Hard Rock or PrizePicks.
  • Track record: every call is graded after the game, so you can see how often Over and Under calls are right.
  • Badges: 🎂 birthday · 😤 faces last season's team · 🏠 born within 35 miles · 📍 home state.
  • Stats from ESPN. Treat it as one input, not a sure thing.

Penny Blocks are five parlays per sport, built from the board's highest projected overs for one stat each (green first, then yellow). One leg per player, no injured players, same-game legs allowed. They rebuild on every update.
