How a call is made
  • Line: consensus from scoresandodds.com (midpoint of the best over and under).
  • Projected %: 60% season hit rate + 40% last-5 hit rate. Green = 55%+, red below.
  • NFL matchup: what today's defense allows this season to the player's position for that stat, ranked 1 (toughest) to 32. It nudges Projected % up to 8 points either way.
  • Vegas %: the chance the over price implies. Projected well above it is the better play.
  • Tap a name: career games vs today's opponent. 🔥 = over in 60%+ of 3+ games. Reference only.
  • Injuries: ⚠️ questionable or day-to-day. OUT, doubtful or injured reserve players are greyed out with no call.
  • Penny Blocks tab: five parlays per sport from the top green calls, with links to build them on Hard Rock or PrizePicks.
  • Blue Chips tab: sign in with an emailed link, tap ☆ next to a player on the board to save him, and follow their props and recent results.
  • Track record: every call is graded after the game, so you can see how often green and red calls are right.
  • Badges: 🎂 birthday · 😤 faces last season's team · 🏠 born within 35 miles · 📍 home state.
  • Stats from ESPN. Ignores injuries and matchups, so treat it as one input.

Penny Blocks are five parlays per sport, built from the board's highest projected green calls for one stat each. One leg per player, no injured players, same-game legs allowed. They rebuild on every update.
