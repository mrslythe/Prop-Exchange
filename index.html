How a call is made. Lines come from scoresandodds.com. The line shown is the consensus: the midpoint of the best over and best under on the board. Each of the player's last 5 games (from ESPN game logs, newest on the right) is green if it went over that line and red if it did not. Hit % is the share of those 5 games that went over. Green means 3 or more of 5 went over, so the trend says hit. Red means the trend says miss. vs Line is the 5-game average minus the line. Five games is a small sample and ignores injuries, matchups and minutes, so treat this as one input, not a sure thing.
